About Apna Live TV

Apna Live TV is an independent channel browser by Yasir Tanveer. Its catalogue groups Pakistani entertainment, news, sports, music, kids and regional channels into a simple interface.

You can search channels, save favourites locally, switch themes, and view recently watched channels. Playback is supplied by external stream providers and availability can change.

This website is not affiliated with the broadcasters listed in its catalogue. Channel names and logos belong to their respective owners. Any public deployment must use streams the operator is authorized to distribute.
