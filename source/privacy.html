Privacy information

Favourites, recently watched channel IDs and your theme preference are stored in this browser using local storage. The application does not require an account or include an analytics service.

External services

Channel logos and streams are requested from external providers. These providers receive normal connection information, including your IP address and request metadata. The HLS playback library is bundled with the site.

Advertising

This package contains advertisement placeholders. No advertising network is active by default. If advertising or analytics is enabled later, the operator must update this page and implement any consent controls required for that integration.

Managing local data

Remove individual favourites with their star buttons. Clearing site data in your browser removes all saved preferences and viewing history.

Hosting

The hosting provider processes requests to deliver these pages and may retain operational access logs according to its own policies.
